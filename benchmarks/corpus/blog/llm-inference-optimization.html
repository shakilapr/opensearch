Speed up LLM inference: batching, quantization and the KV cache

Inference for large language models is often bottlenecked by memory bandwidth rather than raw compute

Continuous batching, quantization to 4-bit or 8-bit weights, and a paged key-value cache all reduce cost per token

The post compares GPTQ and AWQ quantization schemes and their accuracy loss

It warns that speculative decoding helps latency but not necessarily throughput.
