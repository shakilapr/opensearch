Training Language Models to Follow Instructions with Human Feedback

We fine-tune large language models using reinforcement learning from human feedback, abbreviated RLHF

A reward model is trained from pairwise human preference comparisons, then a policy is optimized against it with proximal policy optimization (PPO)

This alignment procedure produced InstructGPT, which follows user instructions far more reliably than the base GPT-3 model

Human labelers rank candidate responses, and the reward model learns to predict those rankings.
