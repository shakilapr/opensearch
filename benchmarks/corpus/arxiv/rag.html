Retrieval-Augmented Generation for Knowledge-Intensive NLP Tasks

Retrieval-augmented generation combines a parametric sequence-to-sequence generator with a non-parametric dense retriever index

A dense passage retriever (DPR) fetches relevant passages, which the generator then conditions on to produce grounded output

This hybrid memory approach reduces hallucination on knowledge-intensive tasks such as open-domain question answering

Both the retriever and the generator can be fine-tuned end to end.
